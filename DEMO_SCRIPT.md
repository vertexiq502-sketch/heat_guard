# Suraksha Heat Shield - MVP Demo Script

**Target Duration:** 3-5 Minutes
**Objective:** Demonstrate the end-to-end accountability loop, localized personalization, and risk engine of Heat Guard.

## 1. Introduction (30 seconds)
**Speaker:** "Welcome to Suraksha Heat Shield. Extreme heat is a silent killer for India’s informal workforce. Existing solutions are generalized weather alerts. Our solution provides localized, hyper-personalized heat risk assessments and an automated escalation chain to ensure accountability."

## 2. Worker Personalization Demo (60 seconds)
**Speaker:** "Let's log in as Arjun, a heavy construction worker."
**Action:** Log in with `demo.worker1@heatguard.dev` (Password: `Demo@1234`).
**Speaker:** "Arjun is doing heavy labor in full sun. Our risk engine calculates his effective risk as **ORANGE (High Risk)** based on the NOAA Heat Index adjusted for his specific profile."
**Action:** Show the Home Screen risk indicator and click into 'Recommendations'.
**Speaker:** "He receives localized guidance: 'Work with caution, drink 2 glasses of water every hour.' Now, let's look at Meena, who works at the exact same site but does moderate delivery work."
**Action:** Log out, log in with `demo.worker2@heatguard.dev` (Password: `Demo@1234`).
**Speaker:** "Meena’s risk is only **YELLOW (Caution)**. Same weather, different profile, different risk. This is the power of our personalization engine."

## 3. Alert & Supervisor Demo (60 seconds)
**Speaker:** "What happens when conditions worsen? Let's switch to the Supervisor's view."
**Action:** Log in with `demo.supervisor1@heatguard.dev` (Password: `Demo@1234`).
**Speaker:** "Kiran, the supervisor, has a bird's-eye view of all his sites. He can see workers at risk and active alerts."
**Action:** Click on 'Site 1'.
**Speaker:** "Arjun's risk just hit RED. A critical alert was dispatched to Arjun in Telugu, his preferred language."
**Action:** Show the alert on the dashboard.
**Speaker:** "If Arjun doesn't acknowledge this alert within 15 minutes, it escalates to Kiran. Kiran can issue a Stop Work order directly from this dashboard."

## 4. Authority Demo (30 seconds)
**Speaker:** "Accountability doesn't stop at the supervisor. If Kiran ignores the alert for 30 minutes, it escalates to the District Authority."
**Action:** Log in with `demo.authority1@heatguard.dev` (Password: `Demo@1234`).
**Speaker:** "Dr. Suresh sees the pending escalation. He has a full audit log of the risk breach, the alerts sent, and the lack of response, allowing him to enforce compliance."

## 5. Closing (30 seconds)
**Speaker:** "Suraksha Heat Shield bridges the gap between macro-level weather data and micro-level human safety. With our localized risk engine and automated escalation chain, we ensure that every worker is protected, and every supervisor is held accountable. Thank you."
